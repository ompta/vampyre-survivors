# Vampyre Survivors

A single-file HTML5 canvas survivor game (Vampire Survivors-style). No build step — just open `index.html` in any browser (desktop or phone).

## How to play
Survive **15 minutes** against endless waves of zombies and bats while your weapons attack automatically. Collect XP gems to level up and pick upgrades.

- **Desktop:** `W A S D` / arrow keys to move · `P` pause · `M` mute · `1 2 3` to pick an upgrade
- **Smartphone:** touch & hold anywhere on screen — a floating joystick appears under your finger and steers the player. Tap the pause/mute buttons and the upgrade cards.

## Game rules
- **XP gems expire after 30 seconds** (they fade out during the last 5 seconds).
- **The LICH KING appears every 1 minute.** If the previous Lich King is still alive when the new one rises, the old one is banished.
